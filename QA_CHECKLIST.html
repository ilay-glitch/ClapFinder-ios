This is a full manual pass over Guard Dog for Android. It is written for someone who did not build the app. Every row is one thing you can look at, listen to or feel, and answer yes or no about.

Tap ✓ if it behaves as described, ✗ if it does not. Tap the same mark again to clear it. Your marks, the device box and your notes are saved in this browser, so you can close the page and come back. When you finish, Copy report puts a summary of every failure on your clipboard.

Rows with an amber edge are guard checks: places where a failure means the phone is not really protected, a thief could silence the alarm, or the user is told they are guarded when they are not. A failure there matters more than any other. Sections with an amber chip are full of them.

Before you start

0/8

Read this section first. A few of these decide whether the pass is useful at all.

  • You are testing on a real phone, not an emulator. An emulator has no real motion or proximity sensor, no torch and no vibration motor, so most of this list means nothing there.
  • You have written the phone’s model, Android version and which APK you installed in the box below. A result without a device is not a result.
  • You are using GuardDog-1.0.0-release-test.apk for this pass. The debug APK is slower and is only for reading logs.
  • If you can, run the pass on at least two brands, one of them Samsung or Xiaomi. Those brands close background apps aggressively and section 08 depends on it.
  • You have a second phone that can call this one.
    Second phone
  • You have a pocket you can actually put the phone in: trousers or a jacket, not a bag.
    Pocket
  • Ringer and alarm volume are not at maximum. Set alarm volume to about half so you can tell when the app raises it.
  • You have read the last section, Not bugs. It lists what is deliberately missing or deliberately strict, so you do not spend time reporting it.

Install and first launch

0/8

Uninstall any older copy first, so this is a genuine first run.

  • The APK installs. The app appears in the launcher as Guard Dog.
  • Its icon is the golden puppy in a navy police cap holding a blue shield, on a sky-blue background. Ears hang down; they are never pointed up.
  • On a launcher with round or squircle icons, the dog’s face, cap and shield are all visible. Nothing important is cut off by the shape.
  • Tapping the icon shows a sky-blue launch screen (with the round icon, on Android 12 and newer), then the app’s own splash. There is no white or black flash between them.
  • The splash shows Guard Dog, the line Nobody touches your phone. Ever. 🐶🛡️, the dog with the shield gently bobbing, and blue rings pulsing behind it.
  • A progress bar and Loading N%… fill to 100%. The number only ever goes up.

02

Onboarding

0/9

Shown only on the very first launch. Three steps.

  • Step 1 reads STEP 1 OF 3, shows the waving dog, the card I’ll guard your phone! / Put me on watch — if anyone touches your phone, I bark 🐶🛡️, and a blue Continue button.
  • Step 2 reads STEP 2 OF 3, shows a bell with rings and a small sitting dog, the card If guarding stops, you’ll know / I’ll send a notification if anything interrupts my watch 🔔, and Enable Notifications.
  • Tapping Enable Notifications shows Android’s own notification prompt (Android 13 and newer). On older Android it goes straight to step 3.
  • Choosing Allow moves on to step 3.
  • On a fresh install, choosing Don’t allow also moves on to step 3. The app never blocks you for refusing.
  • Step 3 reads STEP 3 OF 3, shows the dog with the shield, the card Ready to stand guard. / Arm me whenever you leave your phone., and Start.
  • Start opens the home screen on the Don’t Touch tab.
  • Close the app completely and open it again. Onboarding does not appear again, ever.
  • Text in every card fits. Nothing is cut off, overlapping, or running under the button.

22

Not bugs — please do not file these

0/13

These are deliberate, or known and not part of this build. Mark each one once you have read it.

  • Test ads. Ads say Test Ad. Real ad accounts come before release.
  • Test signing. The release-test APK is signed with a development key. It cannot be updated from the Play Store and Play Protect may warn when installing.
  • The screen goes black in Pocket Mode when the sensor is covered. That stops your pocket from tapping buttons. It lights up again when uncovered.
  • You must unlock to disarm from the notification, and the alarm screen does not show over the lock screen. Otherwise a thief could silence it without your PIN.
  • The app turns the alarm volume up to maximum during an alarm, and keeps it there until you disarm. It puts it back afterwards.
  • No PIN to disarm, no timeout, no shake-to-stop. DISARM on the unlocked phone is the only way out.
  • High sensitivity can go off from table vibration. Known. Section 09 collects data for it.
  • Pocket Mode does not send the three “Motion detected!” notifications. Only Don’t Touch does.
  • No sensitivity setting in Pocket Mode. Not in this version.
  • The “one quick thing” notification explanation before the first arm does not appear, because onboarding already asked about notifications.
  • Portrait only, light look only, English only.
  • The launch ad often does not appear on slow networks. It gives up after 5 seconds by design.
  • Analytics only go to the phone’s log in this build. Nothing is sent anywhere.
